Flickleaf

One word at a time, at the pace of your hand.

Turn on the Safari extension

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Tap Flickleaf and turn it on. Under Permissions, allow Other Websites.
  3. In Safari, open an article and tap the page menu button beside the address. Choose Flickleaf, then Read this page.

Swipe up and down on the reader to move through the words. Flickleaf works in Safari, not in other browsers on iPhone or iPad.

Flickleaf is off in Safari.

  1. Click the button below, or open Safari → Settings → Extensions.
  2. Tick Flickleaf. When Safari asks, allow it on the sites you read.
  3. On an article, click the Flickleaf leaf in the toolbar, then Read this page.
